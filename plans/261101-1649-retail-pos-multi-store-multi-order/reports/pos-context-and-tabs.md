# POS context selection and compact chrome

## Outcome
- Compact blue header and single-line invoice tabs; preserve >=44px pointer/touch targets.
- On login/restore, use validated saved context; otherwise automatically select the sole eligible branch/register. Prompt in a modal only when a choice remains. Empty-register branches hide the register control and do not require it.
- Tabs display stable local `Hóa đơn N` labels, allocated from the highest number among still-open tabs, not server order identifiers. Keep identifiers unchanged for requests and recovery.

## Contract and safety
- Branch-only shifts require an authorized branch with no configured registers. Each staff member owns at most one open branch-only shift per branch; existing register shifts retain their existing uniqueness and authorization.
- Make shift/workspace `register_id` nullable without rewriting historical data. Keep branch, shift, owner, lease, revision, idempotency, totals and financial evidence mandatory.
- Do not auto-open shifts or manufacture registers. Automatic selection is read-only and never claims a workspace lease.
- Do not infer singleton/zero from a filtered or partial page. Selection options are server-authorized; failed requests do not mean there are no registers.
